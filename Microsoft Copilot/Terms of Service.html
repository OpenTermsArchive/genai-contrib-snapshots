Frequently Asked Questions

  • The Copilot consumer app is a conversational chat experience. Microsoft 365 Copilot for consumers brings Copilot to apps like Word, PowerPoint, Excel and Outlook.
  • There are several types of functionalities today including the ability to reference a file to begin a document or presentation in Word and PowerPoint respectively, as well as the ability to generate an image with Designer directly from your Word or PowerPoint canvas from the Copilot chat panels. We are continuing to evolve how Copilot acts across your Microsoft 365 apps as consumers begin to leverage Copilot.
  • Copilot brings the power of AI to the Microsoft 365 apps you use every day to unleash your creativity, unlock your potential, and communicate with confidence. With Copilot, you can effortlessly plan your next trip or event with OneNote, polish up your resume and cover letter in Word, quickly write great emails with the right tone and catch up on long email threads in Outlook, or quickly produce slides for your next PowerPoint party. Copilot is your AI companion to work alongside you, taking care of the busy work so you can stay in the flow and make time for things that spark joy.
  • You can manage your Microsoft 365 subscription by logging into your account, or viewing your account dashboard if you’re already signed in. Learn more about managing your subscriptions.
  • AI features in Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Outlook and Designer included in Microsoft 365 Family plans are only available to the subscription owner and can not be shared with others.
  • Yes, the best value for college students is our discounted version of Microsoft 365 Personal, which provides access to our powerful productivity apps, now infused with Copilot in Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Outlook, and OneNote, along benefits like spacious cloud storage. Learn more about our student offers.
  • With Copilot, you can conveniently tap into the power of AI in the Microsoft 365 apps that you use every day, without the need to toggle between screens or tabs, making it a more seamless experience. Copilot can even apply formatting to your content in the Microsoft 365 apps, taking advantage of hundreds of features many people rarely use. And users can share and collaborate in real-time on content generated by Copilot in apps like Word, Excel, and PowerPoint.
