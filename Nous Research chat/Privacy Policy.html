Nous Research Privacy Policy_

Last Updated: June 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy (“Policy”) describes the kind of information Nous Research (“Nous,” “we,” or “us,” or “our”) may collect or otherwise process during your visit to or use of our websites and tools, including:

  • The Nous Portal platform;
  • Hermes and other AI models, agents, and inference tools made available by Nous;
  • Nous APIs, developer tools, and related integrations;
  • Our websites, including nousresearch.com and related subdomains;
  • Community, support, research, and account management interactions; and
  • Related online and offline interactions with Nous and our products and services

(collectively, the “Services”).

By using the Services, you agree to our collection and use of personal information as described in this Policy and in our Terms of Service.

This Privacy Policy does not cover the personal information we collect about employees and independent contractors or job applicants.

Personal Information We Collect – Sources and Types

When you interact with our Services, we collect your personal information from you when you visit our websites, request information from us, create an account, submit prompts, inputs, or other content, interact with our AI models or APIs, make purchases, or otherwise interact with us through the Services.

The information we collect through the Services may include:

  • Personal identifiers and account information, such as your name, username, email address, telephone number, billing address, account credentials, payment-related information, organization or company affiliation, and other information you choose to provide when creating or managing an account or communicating with us;
  • Commercial and transaction information, such as records of subscriptions, purchases, payment history, account activity, pricing tier selections, and related transaction details;
  • AI workflow data, such as prompts, instructions, messages, uploads, inputs, outputs, responses, conversation history, tool interactions, inference requests, API calls, telemetry, usage logs, and related interaction data generated through your use of the Services;
  • AI usage data, such as records regarding how models, agents, tools, or workflows interact with prompts, systems, APIs, environments, or tools, including intermediate actions, tool usage patterns, execution metadata, and related operational or diagnostic information generated in connection with the Services;
  • Internet or other electronic activity information, such as your IP address, browser type, device identifiers, operating system, device characteristics, referral URLs, interaction with our Services, approximate location derived from IP address, pages viewed, links clicked, session activity, and information regarding your interaction with advertisements or analytics tools;
  • Communications and support information, such as the contents of communications you send to us, support inquiries, survey responses, feedback, bug reports, community interactions, and related records; and
  • Audio, visual, or similar information, such as profile images, recordings of support interactions, or other content you choose to provide.

We may also collect information from third-party services, integrations, analytics providers, payment processors, authentication providers, and infrastructure providers in connection with your use of the Services.

How We Use Your Personal Information

We use the personal information we obtain through the Services to provide, operate, maintain, support, and improve the Services and related products, features, and functionality; to provide you with the information, products, and services you request or purchase; to authenticate users and administer accounts; to process transactions and payments; and to communicate with you regarding your account, your use of the Services, customer support matters, required notices, updates, security alerts, and other administrative or operational communications. We may also use your personal information to provide advertisements, marketing communications, newsletters, promotional materials, and information about products, services, research initiatives, or offerings that may be of interest to you, including those offered by us or our affiliates.

We also use personal information about your use of our Services to monitor and improve our Services; for internal business analysis; to prevent fraud, activities that violate our Terms of Service or that are illegal; and to protect our rights and the rights and safety of our users or others.

Disclosures of Your Personal Information

We may disclose personal information we collect through the Services with service providers that help us operate our business, including through payment processing, verifying customer information, managing customer information and providing customer service, facilitating email communications, providing security services and cloud-based data storage, hosting our websites and assistance with other IT-related functions, advertising and marketing of our products and those of our affiliates, and providing analytics information. We may also disclose personal information with providers that support the operation, development, evaluation, training, fine-tuning, testing, or improvement of our artificial intelligence and machine learning technologies and related products and services.

Additionally, we may disclose personal information and Service-related data, including prompts, inputs, outputs, usage data, telemetry, trace data, and related interaction information, with affiliates, business partners, research collaborators, infrastructure providers, analytics providers, model providers, and other third parties for purposes consistent with this Privacy Policy, including to provide, operate, improve, develop, analyze, train, fine-tune, evaluate, secure, and support the Services and related technologies. We may also generate, use, disclose, license, distribute, or otherwise make available aggregated, anonymized, de-identified, synthetic, derivative, or similar data, datasets, analytics, insights, models, or related materials generated from use of the Services for research, analytics, commercial, operational, product development, artificial intelligence training, or other lawful business purposes, provided such information is not intended to identify particular users.

We may also disclose your personal information as required or permitted by law to comply with a subpoena or similar legal process or government request, or when we believe in good faith that disclosure is legally required or otherwise necessary to protect our or others’ rights, property, or safety, including to law enforcement agencies, and judicial and regulatory authorities. Additionally, we may disclose your personal information to third parties to help detect and protect against fraud or data security vulnerabilities. And we may transfer or disclose your personal information to a third party in the event of an actual or proposed sale, merger, reorganization of our entity or other restructuring.

Third-Party AI Models and Service Providers

Certain Services may utilize third-party artificial intelligence models, infrastructure providers, or other service providers to provide requested functionality. As a result, prompts, inputs, outputs, and related information submitted through the Services may be shared with such providers as necessary to provide the Services. These third-party providers process such information in accordance with their own terms, privacy policies, and practices. We encourage users to review the privacy policies of any third-party providers whose services are used in connection with the Services.

Cookies and Analytics

Cookies are small, sometimes encrypted text files that are stored on computer hard drives by websites that you visit. They are used to help users navigate websites efficiently as well as to provide information to the owner of the websites. You may set your browser to refuse cookies or alert you when cookies are being sent; some browsers have other cookie management tools.

When you visit our websites, we may place cookies or other online tracking technologies (e.g., pixels, web beacons) on your browser. Among other things, these help us recognize you when you return to our websites, provide information for analytics, and help us personalize advertisements for you.

We and our analytics providers may use cookies and similar technologies to collect information about how you access and interact with the Services, including referring URLs or sources, pages viewed, links clicked, session activity, browser and device information, approximate location derived from IP address, and related analytics identifiers. We use this information to analyze usage of the Services, maintain and improve the Services, and understand how users arrive at and interact with our websites.

We use Google Analytics to evaluate the use of our website. Google Analytics uses cookies and other identifiers to collect information, such as how often users visit the website, what pages they visit when they do so, and what other websites they visited prior to visiting our website. To learn more about how Google Analytics collects personal information, review Google’s Privacy Policy.

Personal Information of Minors

Our products and services are not directed to minors under the age of 13 and we do not knowingly collect the personal information of minors under 13. If we learn that we have collected personal information from a child under 13, we will take reasonable steps to delete that information or deactivate the account, as appropriate.

How We Keep Your Personal Information Secure

We implement and maintain reasonable security measures appropriate to the nature of the personal information that we collect, use, retain, transfer or otherwise process. Those measures include administrative, physical, and technical safeguards to protect the security, confidentiality, and integrity of personal information. However, data security incidents and breaches can occur due to a variety of factors that cannot reasonably be prevented; therefore, our safeguards may not always be adequate to prevent all breaches of security.

Your Privacy Choices and Opt-Out Rights

We provide users with the ability to limit certain uses of their personal information through the privacy controls made available within the Services. In particular, users may elect to enable “Privacy Mode,” which allows users to opt out of the storage and use of certain inference payloads for training, product improvement, and support purposes.

To enable Privacy Mode:

  • Log in to your account at portal.nousresearch.com;
  • Navigate to “Account Settings;”
  • Locate the “Privacy Settings” section;
  • Review the available privacy options; and
  • Select “Privacy Mode”

Once Privacy Mode has been selected and the setting has been applied to your account, your preference will be reflected within the Privacy Settings interface. Privacy Mode limits our storage and use of inference payloads, such as prompts, inputs, uploads, messages, outputs, and responses. When Privacy Mode is enabled, we will not store your inference payloads and will not use such inference payloads for training, product improvement, or support purposes, except to the limited extent necessary to maintain the security, integrity, and operation of the Services, comply with applicable law, or detect, prevent, or investigate fraud, abuse, or security incidents. Privacy Mode does not prevent us from collecting, storing, or using operational metadata where needed to provide, route, secure, operate, debug, bill for, or otherwise maintain the Services.

Please note that Privacy Mode may apply only on a prospective basis and may not affect information that was collected, processed, incorporated into models, or otherwise used prior to the activation of Privacy Mode. In addition, certain privacy settings or controls may apply only to particular Services or features. Enabling Privacy Mode may impact the functionality, personalization, or availability of certain features of the Services.

When We Do Not Act on a Request

In some cases, we may not act on your requests (e.g., if we cannot do so in order to comply with applicable law). When this is the case, we will explain our reasons for not providing you with the information or taking the action you requested.

Other Websites and Social Media Buttons

Our websites may contain links to other websites or social media buttons. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or the content of those websites or social media platforms. This Privacy Policy applies only to our business and the personal information we collect.

Changes to This Policy

If we make changes to this Privacy Policy, we post those changes on the website and update the date of the Privacy Policy. If the changes are material, you will be notified via a notice on our website.

Contact Us

If there are any questions regarding this Policy or to request a copy of this Policy in another format you may contact us using the information below.

privacy@nousresearch.com
